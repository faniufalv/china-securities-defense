## Q1: [Criminal Defense] For Expatriates: What legal considerations should overseas Chinese have when buying property in China?

**A**: Overseas Chinese purchasing property in China should note: 1) Confirm purchase eligibility, some cities have restrictions; 2) Overseas income certificates need notarization and authentication; 3) Fund remittance requires foreign exchange registration; 4) Property registration should use Chinese name plus passport number.

**法律依据**: 《城市房地产管理法》

**领域**: 房产纠纷 | **语言**: English

## Q2: For Expatriates: How is property divided in cross-border divorce?

**A**: Cross-border divorce property division involves multiple legal systems: 1) Determine jurisdiction court first; 2) Chinese property follows Chinese law, overseas property may follow local law; 3) Different countries have different standards for marital property; 4) Prenuptial agreements recommended.

**法律依据**: 《民事诉讼法》涉外编

**领域**: 婚姻家事 | **语言**: English
